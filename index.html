Last Stand

Three levels — the goblin warcamp, the undead crypt and the volcanic ruins — built to PRD v1.9 with the v1.6 art for Levels 2 and 3. Every level is four circles on open ground, four on the castle walkway, four heroes rooted in place, and a boss at the end: the Troll King, the Necromancer, then the Dragon. Castle and hero health reset at the start of every level. You commit to a circle before the level starts and you live with it. The castle is the objective. When its health reaches zero the attempt is over on the spot, however many heroes are still standing — a run that ends with three dead and the castle untouched beat a run where everyone lived and the castle is at 40%. This is the level Section 2 says has to land reliably inside a workshop — two attempts and a debrief in about ten minutes.

Controls

Q ability 1  ·  E ability 2 (once per stage — one charge for the whole attempt, boss included)  ·  Shift+E as Support spends Bulwark on the castle instead of the party  ·  1–4 take over a hero  ·  T tuning  ·  Space pause. Piercing Shot asks you to click a lane. Everything else is aimed for you.

What is in this build

Circle selection with no enemy preview, wave spawning with goblins and spear throwers, the stacking soft cap, taunt as a hard global retarget, duration-based Bulwark, the Troll King with Club Swipe, Club Slam, a low-HP enrage and a telegraphed Gate Smash aimed at the castle, the three-state telegraph, wave and boss banners, and an after-action report that leads with castle damage and compares attempt against attempt. Sound is synthesised in the browser — wind-up and impact never share a cue.

New since v1.5: the castle with its own health pool and its own loss condition; melee enemies walking to the gate the moment no living hero holds the front line; melee immunity on the walkway paid for in reach, so the Tank contributes nothing from up there and the Ranger contributes everything; the taunt-redirect rule that stops a Tank on the wall stalling the game; ability 2 as a once-per-stage charge with no wave-transition reset; and one preventable boss siege attack per fight.

New in v2.0: Levels 2 and 3 are playable, with the v1.6 art. The undead crypt (zombies, skeleton archers that hang back, the Necromancer's Poison Orb and Summon) and the volcanic ruins (lava golems, Firebats that fly over the front line at the wall, the Dragon's four-cast Meteor Rain and Fire Spray sweep). The same castle defends all three levels and resets with the heroes at the start of each one. Continue on the victory screen now advances to the next level, and the level buttons on Circle Selection jump straight to any level.

New in v2.1 — animation pass: everything moves at display rate instead of the 20 Hz sim tick, and multiplayer clients now look as smooth as the host. Walk cycles, attack poses and hit reactions on every enemy; deaths that topple, crumble or fall out of the air instead of vanishing; heroes that swing, draw, cast, flinch, fall and rise again; bosses that breathe, wind up and sink when killed; real projectiles with trails; reworked Volley, Piercing Shot, Hammer Slam, Heal, Revive and Bulwark; debris, dust and screen shake on heavy hits; a timer arc on every wind-up; numbers that pop and float; a title card on each level; and softer screen, banner and HUD transitions. Motion: reduced (next to Sound) turns off shake and thins the particles. None of it touches the simulation — harness results are identical to v2.0.

New in v2.2 — hero select: Play solo now opens a hero screen with the PRD 11.2 front-view models, a short bio, health, damage, attack speed and reach, how each hero auto-attacks, and both abilities with their cooldowns. Every number is read live from the tuning drawer. Meet the heroes opens the same screen read-only from the lobby, so multiplayer players can read up on their seat.
